# Publishing Rules

## Company names and job ads

The site may reference a company and public job title to discuss the public brief and present an independently designed solution.

Use a clear disclaimer:
> Based on a public job advertisement. No affiliation, endorsement or commercial relationship is implied.

## Never publish

- invented internal company systems;
- invented policies;
- invented staff numbers unless sourced and necessary;
- fake screenshots;
- fake usage or ROI;
- claims that our system is deployed at the company;
- private contact information;
- copied full job descriptions.

## Copy discipline

Paraphrase the brief. Link to the original source. Keep quotation to a minimum.

## Freshness

Every page carries `verified_on`.
Before an application is sent, re-check the source. If the role has closed, the page can remain as research but should say the listing is no longer verified as open.
