# First Build Order

Build these pages first. They give the strongest range of employer problems while reusing the same core technology.

## 1. ABC — AI Adoption Specialist
Live, direct employer source, and the brief is almost a perfect statement of the product thesis.

## 2. The Arnott's Group — AI Enablement and Adoption Lead
Direct employer brief gives an unusually complete specification: workflow friction, Copilot, capability, communities, governance and value.

## 3. Findex — AI & Digital Tools Adoption Lead
Explicitly asks for tool SME, use-case library, agents/workflows, community, governance and executive impact.

## 4. Bellroy — AI Enablement Lead
Excellent proof target because it explicitly wants real workflow problem-solving, judgement about when not to use AI, licence ROI and living guidance.

## 5. CBRE Asia Pacific — AI Adoption Lead
Hands-on adoption role: sit with teams, run sessions, communicate, see what works and fix what does not.

## 6. Icon Group — Project Trainer - National Heidi AI Adoption
A very concrete rollout problem across 50+ sites, ideal for showing real implementation/adoption logic rather than generic AI advice.

## 7. Novigi — AI Adoption Coach
Their ad explicitly rejects licence-rollout/lunch-and-learn adoption and wants real work to change.

## 8. Insentra — AI Adoption Lead
Shows the consulting/managed transformation version: executive alignment through sustained measurable outcomes.

## 9. David Jones — AI Capability & Enablement Lead
Large-workforce role-based capability and measurable impact across store and corporate environments.

## 10. Deloitte Australia — AI Value & Adoption Lead - Director
Shows the executive value-realisation layer and proves this category goes beyond training into strategic portfolio accountability.

## Rule

A page is not "done" because the copy looks good. It is done only when:
- the public brief is fact-checked;
- the custom solution is reviewed;
- at least one real AI action works;
- the action never invents company data;
- source and disclaimer are visible;
- mobile and desktop QA pass.
