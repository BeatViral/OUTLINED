# Product Spec

## Core idea

Do not ask a company to create an account.

The company arrives because it has advertised an AI-related role. It sees its company and role on the site, clicks it, and receives a custom solution derived from the public role brief.

This is not a generic AI platform page. Each role gets a deliberately configured solution.

## What the visitor should feel

"I can see that this person understood exactly what we are trying to achieve, and has already designed the operating system/tools around it."

## Product loop

1. Discover a public AI adoption / enablement / training / governance / transformation job.
2. Capture the public source and verified date.
3. Extract only what the ad actually supports.
4. Separate:
   - stated company need;
   - our inference;
   - our proposed solution.
5. Select reusable modules.
6. Add company/industry-specific logic.
7. Human review.
8. Publish page.
9. Real AI action available on page.
10. Re-check job source before sending the application.

## Shared technology, custom solution

The underlying modules are reusable, but the visitor should never feel like they are seeing a generic module catalogue.

For example:
- ABC: editorial/business workflow adoption and safe practical coaching.
- Icon Group: Heidi AI training, site rollout, go-live support and adoption.
- CBA: change/delivery AI enablement, capability and responsible execution.
- Bellroy: cross-functional workflow diagnosis plus tool/licence value.
- Law firm: legal workflow, matter-safe use, lawyer support and legal AI patterns.
